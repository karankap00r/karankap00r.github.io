AI, creative writing, sketching, MMORPGs, crypto, web development, a Master's application made out of FOMO — the pattern was excitement about everything and depth in nothing. The post borrows the diversification argument from investing, then turns it around: spread across too many things, nothing compounds. What follows is the split that stuck — about a tenth of the time going broad to know what exists, the rest going deep on one thing until it pays.
