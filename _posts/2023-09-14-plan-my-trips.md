A family trip to Vaishno Devi — a promise to my mother after graduation — started with hauling luggage from hotel to hotel looking for one that would take us. That morning turned into a system: the attributes every trip has (purpose, travel to and within, visa, stay, food, the apps locals actually use) and the order to settle them in so the next trip's worst moment is smaller than this one's.
