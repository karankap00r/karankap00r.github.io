Codebases turn into legacy faster than they used to: a few quarters of shipping is enough. The first wiki was an API contract for a mobile integration; the habit grew from there into architecture notes, runbooks, and onboarding guides. The post walks through why documentation gets dropped, what a hundred wikis actually did for onboarding and incident response, and how to make writing them a normal part of shipping rather than an afterthought.
