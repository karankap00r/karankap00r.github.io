Competitive programming teaches you to solve a well-defined problem alone. The job turned out to be the opposite: figuring out how systems owned by other teams fit together, getting unstuck on problems that were as often organisational as technical, and surviving the breadth of everything you do not yet know. The post is a list of what the first year actually taught — and the reassurance that nobody is quizzing you.
