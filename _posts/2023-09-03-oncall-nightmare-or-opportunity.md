Late-night pages and outages with no obvious cause make on-call the week everyone avoids. The argument here is that it is the best onboarding an engineer gets: no project exposes as much of a system, its dependencies, and its failure modes as a rotation does. The post covers what a rotation teaches that feature work cannot, how to prepare before the pager is yours, and how to run an incident without losing the plot — from a first on-call in 2017 onward.
